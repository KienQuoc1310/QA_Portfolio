# BUG-003 — Product Price Is Inconsistent Between Cart and Checkout

## 1. Bug Information

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Bug ID | BUG-003 |
| Module | Cart / Checkout |
| Related Test Cases | TC-CART-029, TC-CHK-019 |
| Severity | High |
| Priority | High |
| Environment | Web |
| Browser | Chrome |
| Platform | Windows |
| Status | New |
| Reproducibility | 100% |

---

## 2. Summary

The product price displayed in Checkout is different from the price displayed for the same product in Cart.

---

## 3. Preconditions

- Product is available.
- Product has a defined price.
- User can add products to Cart.
- User can access Checkout.
- No discount or promotion is applied.
- No shipping fee is included in the product price.

---

## 4. Test Data

| Item | Value |
|---|---:|
| Product | Product A |
| Product Price | $100 |
| Quantity | 2 |
| Cart Subtotal | $200 |
| Checkout Unit Price | $120 |
| Checkout Subtotal | $240 |

---

## 5. Steps to Reproduce

1. Open the E-commerce Web Application.
2. Open Product A.
3. Verify that Product A price is $100.
4. Add 2 units of Product A to the Cart.
5. Open the Cart.
6. Verify that Product A price is displayed as $100.
7. Verify that the Cart subtotal is $200.
8. Click Checkout.
9. Review Product A in the Checkout order summary.

---

## 6. Expected Result

The product information should remain consistent between Cart and Checkout.

Expected:

- Product A unit price = $100.
- Quantity = 2.
- Subtotal = $200.

Checkout should use the applicable price defined by the business rules.

---

## 7. Actual Result

Checkout displays:

- Product A unit price = $120.
- Quantity = 2.
- Subtotal = $240.

The price is inconsistent with the price previously displayed in Cart.

---

## 8. Reproducibility

The issue can be reproduced consistently.

Reproduction rate:

**5/5 attempts**

---

## 9. Impact

The inconsistency can cause:

- Customer confusion.
- Incorrect order expectations.
- Incorrect order totals.
- Potential financial disputes.
- Loss of customer trust.

---

## 10. Evidence

Attach:

- Product page screenshot.
- Cart screenshot.
- Checkout screenshot.
- Screen recording showing the transition from Product → Cart → Checkout.

---

## 11. Suggested Investigation Area

Check the data passed between:

- Product page.
- Cart.
- Checkout.
- Pricing service.
- Order calculation logic.

Verify whether the same product identifier and applicable price are used throughout the flow.
